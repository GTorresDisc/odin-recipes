Home

Description

To make a homemade pizza, start by preheating your oven to 475°F (245°C). Roll out your pizza dough on a lightly floured surface and place it on a baking tray or pizza stone. Spread a thin layer of tomato sauce evenly over the dough, leaving a small border around the edges. Sprinkle shredded mozzarella cheese on top, then add your favorite toppings, such as pepperoni, mushrooms, onions, bell peppers, or olives. Bake the pizza for 12–15 minutes, or until the crust is golden brown and the cheese is melted and bubbly. Remove the pizza from the oven, let it cool for a few minutes, then slice and serve while hot.

Ingredients

  • Pizza dough
  • Tomato sauce
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Pepperoni
  • Olive oil

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven.
  2. Roll out the pizza dough.
  3. Spread the tomato sauce on the dough.
  4. Add the mozzarella cheese.
  5. Add the pepperoni.
  6. Bake the pizza until the crust is golden and the cheese is melted.
  7. Let it cool for a few minutes, then slice and serve.
