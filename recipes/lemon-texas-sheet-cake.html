Lemon Texas Sheet Cake

Home

Description

Lemon Texas Sheet Cake is a light, moist dessert with a bright lemon flavor. It is baked in a large, shallow pan and topped with a sweet lemon glaze that adds a refreshing citrus taste. This cake is easy to make and is perfect for parties, family gatherings, or any occasion that calls for a simple and delicious dessert.

Ingredients

  • Flour
  • Sugar
  • Butter
  • Eggs
  • Lemon juice
  • Lemon zest
  • Baking powder
  • Powdered sugar

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven.
  2. Mix the ingredients together.
  3. Pour the batter into a baking pan.
  4. Bake until the cake is done.
  5. Make the lemon glaze.
  6. Pour the glaze over the warm cake.
  7. Let it cool and serve.
